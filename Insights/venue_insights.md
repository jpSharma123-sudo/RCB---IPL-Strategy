# 🏟️ RCB Venue Performance Insights

## 📌 Objective

This analysis evaluates RCB's performance across different IPL
venues during the 2013–2016 period.

The objective is to identify venues where RCB performed strongly,
understand venue-specific performance patterns and support
venue-based player selection for the 2017 Mega Auction.

## 🎯 Strategic Implications

Venue-level analysis can support RCB's squad-building strategy by:

- Identifying players suited to specific playing conditions.
- Understanding home-ground advantage.
- Planning bowling combinations according to venue characteristics.
- Selecting players with consistent performance across different venues.
- Supporting match-specific player selection.

---

## 💡 Business Insight

Venue performance should be considered alongside player consistency,
batting and bowling performance when making player-selection decisions.

A player with strong overall statistics may not necessarily be the
best choice for every venue. Venue suitability can therefore provide
an additional factor for optimizing squad composition.

---

## 🏆 Auction Relevance

Venue analysis can help RCB prioritize players who can perform
effectively under different playing conditions.

The final auction recommendation will combine venue suitability
with player performance, consistency and overall squad requirements.
