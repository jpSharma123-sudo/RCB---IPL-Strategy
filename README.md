# 🏏 RCB IPL Performance & Mega Auction Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive SQL-driven analysis of 
Royal Challengers Bangalore (RCB)'s IPL performance from 2013 
to 2016, with the objective of supporting data-driven player 
selection and investment decisions for the 2017 Mega Player Auction.

The analysis focuses on player performance, team performance, 
venue trends, toss decisions, bowling styles, consistency, 
versatility and other strategic KPIs.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective is to identify high-performing and reliable 
players while determining the areas where RCB should prioritize 
investment during the 2017 Mega Auction.

The analysis aims to:

- Evaluate RCB's season-wise performance
- Identify high-performing batsmen and bowlers
- Measure player consistency across seasons
- Analyze venue-specific player and team performance
- Evaluate the impact of toss decisions on match outcomes
- Analyze the relationship between bowling style and wickets
- Identify versatile players contributing with both bat and ball
- Assess home-ground advantage
- Identify gaps in RCB's overall team performance
- Develop data-driven auction recommendations

## 📊 Key Findings

### RCB Season Performance

RCB's batting performance reached its highest level in 2016,
while bowling performance showed comparatively less consistent
improvement across the analyzed seasons.

### Player Performance

The analysis identifies players with strong batting performance,
bowling contribution and all-round capabilities for potential
auction consideration.

### Venue Performance

M Chinnaswamy Stadium showed a strong home record for RCB and
represents an important factor in squad and match strategy.

### Bowling Gap

The analysis indicates that RCB's bowling depth and consistency
represented a recurring strategic area for improvement.

---

## 💡 Auction Strategy

Based on the analysis, RCB should:

1. Prioritize bowling depth.
2. Target consistent wicket-taking bowlers.
3. Consider versatile all-rounders.
4. Retain proven performers with strong recent-season form.
5. Consider venue-specific player suitability.
6. Avoid over-investing in areas where existing batting strength
   already provides sufficient depth.
7. Balance player performance with strategic team requirements.
